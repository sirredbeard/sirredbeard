Hello! I am Hayden Barnes. I am a technical product director, partnership manager, community builder, developer relations professional, and experienced engineering manager. Currently Senior Open Source Partner Manager at [HeroDevs](https://herodevs.com/), where I lead Never-Ending Support (NES) for .NET and do other interesting thing.

I am a six-time [Microsoft MVP](https://mvp.microsoft.com) (2020–2025) with a focus on Windows Subsystem for Linux (WSL) and cross-platform/cross-architecture development, and an [Arm Ambassador](https://www.arm.com/resources/education/ambassador-program).

## WSL

I founded [Whitewater Foundry](https://github.com/WhitewaterFoundry) and created Pengwin, the first custom Linux distribution built for WSL. Pengwin was featured in the **Windows Terminal** demo at Microsoft Build 2019. I later organized [WSLConf](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=wslconf), the first community conference dedicated to WSL. I also maintain the [WSL Community Telegram](https://t.me/+SK14TGZ7O2bYtRyo). 

I am a Contributor to [microsoft/WSL](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL), with the [first community pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL/pull/12924) merged into the project and engaged on [245+ issues](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL/issues?q=commenter%3Asirredbeard).
